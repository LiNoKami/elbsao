Illfang the Kobold Lord

A named boss with several gear associations in the client.

OverviewEncounterLoot and gear

Encounter

Illfang is listed among the client’s boss entities. The beta encounter’s entrance, phases and party requirements still need confirmation.

Client loot rules associate Illfang with Kobold Lord’s Phantom, Usurper’s Buckler and Warlord’s Horn. Live availability and drop rates are unknown.
