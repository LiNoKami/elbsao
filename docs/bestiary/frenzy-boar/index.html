Mob

Frenzy Boar

Listen for the hoof scrape and snort. The charge is the encounter’s most recognizable cue.

OverviewEncounterLoot and gear
Type
Mob
Identifying cues
Hoof scrape, snort, charge
Associated gear
Tuskbite Ring, Boarfang Blade

Encounter

The client includes distinct sound cues for scraping, snorting, charging, skidding, and crashing. On the beta server, the timing, reach, and damage of these actions still need confirmation.

Boarrun Dell is a named biome in the client. Frenzy Boar spawn locations still need to be verified in game.

Loot and gear

The client links a Tuskbite Ring loot rule to the Frenzy Boar. The ring’s availability and drop rate on the beta server are unknown.

Frenzy Boar Tusks are listed as a material in the Boarfang Blade recipe.
